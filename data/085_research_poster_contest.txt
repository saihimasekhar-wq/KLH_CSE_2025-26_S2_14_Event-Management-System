RESEARCH POSTER CONTEST

Research Poster Contest is a college event organized to encourage student participation and practical learning.
The event brings students together through activities, competitions, demonstrations, and interactive sessions related to research poster contest.

Event Date: November 75, 2026
Venue: Sports Ground
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can register individually or in teams and participate in the activities planned by the organizing committee.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, schedules, participant coordination, and venue arrangements.
The event also includes opportunities for students to interact, demonstrate their skills, and learn from other participants.

Research Poster Contest provides students with an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork, creativity, and practical skills.
