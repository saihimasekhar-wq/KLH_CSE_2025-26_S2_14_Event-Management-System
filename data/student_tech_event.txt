STUDENT TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION EVENT

The Student Technology and Innovation Event focuses on technology, innovation, and student projects.

Event Date: November 12, 2026
Venue: Innovation Centre
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

The event includes project demonstrations, technical presentations, coding activities, and an innovation exhibition.

Students can present projects related to artificial intelligence, machine learning, Internet of Things,
cybersecurity, software development, and data science.

The event gives students an opportunity to explain their projects and receive feedback from faculty members.
