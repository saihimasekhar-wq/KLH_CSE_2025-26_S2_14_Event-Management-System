STARTUP IDEA PITCH 2026

Startup Idea Pitch is an entrepreneurship event for students with innovative business ideas.
Participants present their startup concepts to a panel and explain the problem, solution, and business model.

Event Date: October 26, 2026
Venue: Business School Auditorium
Time: 2:00 PM onwards

Students can participate in individual or team pitches and present prototypes or business plans.
The entrepreneurship committee will manage registrations, presentation order, and judging.
The event also includes a networking session for participants.

Startup Idea Pitch provides students with an opportunity to develop communication, business, and presentation skills.
