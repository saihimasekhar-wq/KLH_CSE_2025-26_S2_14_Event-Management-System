HACKATHON 2026

The Hackathon is an intensive team-based event where students develop technology solutions.
Teams work on practical problems and create working prototypes within the event period.

Event Date: October 24, 2026
Venue: Innovation Center
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Participants can develop applications in areas such as education, healthcare, sustainability, and campus services.
The hackathon committee will manage team registration, mentors, judging, and technical support.
The event also includes prototype demonstrations and final presentations.

The Hackathon provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate creativity, collaboration, and rapid development skills.
