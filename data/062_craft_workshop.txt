CRAFT WORKSHOP

Craft Workshop is a college event organized to encourage student participation and practical learning.
The event brings students together through activities, competitions, demonstrations, and interactive sessions related to craft workshop.

Event Date: November 52, 2026
Venue: Seminar Hall A
Time: 11:00 AM onwards

Students can register individually or in teams and participate in the activities planned by the organizing committee.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, schedules, participant coordination, and venue arrangements.
The event also includes opportunities for students to interact, demonstrate their skills, and learn from other participants.

Craft Workshop provides students with an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork, creativity, and practical skills.
