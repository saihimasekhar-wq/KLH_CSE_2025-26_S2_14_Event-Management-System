FRESHERS WELCOME EVENT 2026

The Freshers Welcome Event is organized to welcome new students to the college community.

Event Date: August 5, 2026
Venue: Main Auditorium
Time: 4:00 PM onwards

The event includes an introduction session, cultural performances, games, music, and interaction
between senior and junior students.

Students can participate in welcome activities and cultural performances.
The organizing committee will manage seating, registration, and event coordination.

The Freshers Welcome Event helps new students become familiar with the college environment.
