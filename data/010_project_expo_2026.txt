PROJECT EXPO 2026

Project Expo is an exhibition where students present academic and technical projects.
The expo allows visitors to understand student work through demonstrations and presentations.

Event Date: October 28, 2026
Venue: Project Exhibition Hall
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can display software applications, hardware prototypes, research projects, and innovative models.
The project committee will manage registration, display spaces, and evaluation schedules.
The expo also includes a project poster section.

Project Expo provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate technical knowledge, creativity, and presentation skills.
