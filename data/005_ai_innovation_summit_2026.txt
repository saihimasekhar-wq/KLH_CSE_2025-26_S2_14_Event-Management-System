AI INNOVATION SUMMIT 2026

The AI Innovation Summit introduces students to applications of artificial intelligence.
The summit brings students together for talks, demonstrations, and discussions on AI technologies.

Event Date: October 18, 2026
Venue: Seminar Hall A
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can attend expert sessions, participate in an AI quiz, and present mini projects.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, speaker sessions, and presentation schedules.
The event also includes an AI project showcase.

The summit provides students with an opportunity to explore artificial intelligence and develop innovative ideas.
