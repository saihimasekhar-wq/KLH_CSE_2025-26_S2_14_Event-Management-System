TECHNICAL SYMPOSIUM 2026

The Technical Symposium is a technical event conducted for students interested in technology,
innovation, programming, and engineering.

Event Date: October 15, 2026
Venue: H Block Seminar Hall
Time: 9:30 AM onwards

The event includes coding contests, paper presentations, project demonstrations, technical quizzes,
and an innovation challenge.

Students can register individually or as teams.
The technical committee will evaluate projects and presentations.

The Technical Symposium encourages students to develop problem-solving skills and present innovative ideas.
