SMART CAMPUS HACKATHON 2026

The Smart Campus Hackathon is a programming and innovation event where students develop solutions
for real-world campus problems.

Event Date: November 2, 2026
Venue: Innovation Lab
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Teams will work on problems related to smart classrooms, campus navigation, event management,
energy efficiency, and student services.

The hackathon includes problem statement selection, development, mentor reviews, and final project presentations.

Students are encouraged to use software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing,
and data structures to develop their solutions.
