ANNUAL CULTURAL FESTIVAL 2026

The Annual Cultural Festival is one of the major events organized by the college.
The festival brings students together through music, dance, drama, art, and cultural activities.

Event Date: October 10, 2026
Venue: Main Auditorium
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can participate in solo dance, group dance, singing, drama, and traditional performances.
The cultural committee will manage registrations and stage arrangements.
The festival also includes a food court and exhibition area.

The Annual Cultural Festival provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate creativity,
teamwork, and cultural talent.
