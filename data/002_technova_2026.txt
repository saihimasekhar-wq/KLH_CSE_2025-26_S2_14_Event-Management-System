TECHNOVA 2026

TechNova is a technical festival designed to encourage innovation among students.
The event brings students together through technology competitions, demonstrations, and technical activities.

Event Date: October 12, 2026
Venue: Innovation Hall
Time: 9:30 AM onwards

Students can participate in coding contests, project demonstrations, robotics challenges, and technical quizzes.
The technical committee will manage registrations, competition schedules, and venue arrangements.
The festival also includes technology stalls and project exhibition areas.

TechNova provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate problem-solving, innovation, teamwork, and technical skills.
