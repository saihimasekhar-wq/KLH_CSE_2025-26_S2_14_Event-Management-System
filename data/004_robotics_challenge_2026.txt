ROBOTICS CHALLENGE 2026

The Robotics Challenge is a hands-on technical event focused on robotics and automation.
Students work with robots and sensors to complete practical challenges.

Event Date: October 16, 2026
Venue: Robotics Arena
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Participants can compete in line following, obstacle navigation, and robot control activities.
The robotics committee will manage registrations, equipment, and competition rounds.
A demonstration zone will allow students to present their robotic projects.

The Robotics Challenge provides students with an opportunity to develop engineering, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.
