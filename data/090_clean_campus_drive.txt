CLEAN CAMPUS DRIVE

Clean Campus Drive is a college event organized to encourage student participation and practical learning.
The event brings students together through activities, competitions, demonstrations, and interactive sessions related to clean campus drive.

Event Date: November 80, 2026
Venue: Workshop Lab
Time: 9:30 AM onwards

Students can register individually or in teams and participate in the activities planned by the organizing committee.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, schedules, participant coordination, and venue arrangements.
The event also includes opportunities for students to interact, demonstrate their skills, and learn from other participants.

Clean Campus Drive provides students with an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork, creativity, and practical skills.
