SANKRANTI FESTIVAL 2027

The Sankranti Festival 2027 is a cultural festival organized by the college for students and staff.
The event brings students together through traditional performances, music, dance, decorations, and festive activities.

Event Date: January 14, 2027
Venue: College Courtyard
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Students can participate in cultural performances, traditional games, decoration contests, music, dance, and other festive activities.
The cultural committee will manage registrations, decorations, stage arrangements, and event coordination.
The festival also includes a cultural exhibition and refreshments area.

The Sankranti Festival 2027 provides students with an opportunity to celebrate cultural traditions,
develop teamwork and creativity, and enjoy a sense of community and togetherness.
