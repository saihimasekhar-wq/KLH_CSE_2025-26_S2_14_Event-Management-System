ANNUAL SPORTS MEET 2026

The Annual Sports Meet is a major college event organized to encourage physical activity and sportsmanship.
The event brings students together through individual and team sports competitions.

Event Date: November 20, 2026
Venue: College Sports Ground
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Students can participate in athletics, cricket, football, volleyball, badminton, and other sports.
The sports committee will manage registrations, fixtures, equipment, and ground arrangements.
The event also includes an opening ceremony and prize distribution.

The Annual Sports Meet provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate fitness,
teamwork, discipline, and competitive spirit.
