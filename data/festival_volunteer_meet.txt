FESTIVAL VOLUNTEER MEETING

A volunteer meeting will be conducted before the Annual Cultural Festival.

Meeting Date: October 5, 2026
Venue: Seminar Hall 2
Time: 2:00 PM

Volunteers will receive responsibilities for registration, seating management, stage management,
guest coordination, and crowd management.

The festival coordinator will explain the event schedule and volunteer duties.
All registered volunteers should attend the meeting.

The volunteer team will work together to ensure that the festival activities are conducted smoothly.
