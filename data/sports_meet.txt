INTER-COLLEGE SPORTS MEET 2026

The Inter-College Sports Meet is a sports event conducted for students from different colleges.

Event Date: October 20, 2026
Venue: College Sports Ground
Time: 8:00 AM onwards

The sports meet includes cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, athletics, and chess.

Students must complete registration before participating.
The sports committee will prepare match schedules and allocate teams to different grounds.

The event promotes teamwork, discipline, fitness, and competitive spirit.
