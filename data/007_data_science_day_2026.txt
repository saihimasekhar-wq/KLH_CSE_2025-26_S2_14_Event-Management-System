DATA SCIENCE DAY 2026

Data Science Day is an academic event focused on data analysis and machine learning.
The event brings students together to explore datasets, analytical methods, and practical applications.

Event Date: October 22, 2026
Venue: Data Analytics Lab
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can participate in data challenges, visualization contests, and machine learning demonstrations.
The data science committee will manage registrations, datasets, and lab arrangements.
A poster exhibition will showcase student analytics projects.

Data Science Day provides students with an opportunity to develop analytical thinking and data-driven problem-solving skills.
