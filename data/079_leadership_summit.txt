LEADERSHIP SUMMIT

Leadership Summit is a college event organized to encourage student participation and practical learning.
The event brings students together through activities, competitions, demonstrations, and interactive sessions related to leadership summit.

Event Date: November 69, 2026
Venue: Innovation Center
Time: 10:00 AM onwards

Students can register individually or in teams and participate in the activities planned by the organizing committee.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, schedules, participant coordination, and venue arrangements.
The event also includes opportunities for students to interact, demonstrate their skills, and learn from other participants.

Leadership Summit provides students with an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork, creativity, and practical skills.
