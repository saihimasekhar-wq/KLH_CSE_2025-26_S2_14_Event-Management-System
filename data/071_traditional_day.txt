TRADITIONAL DAY

Traditional Day is a college event organized to encourage student participation and practical learning.
The event brings students together through activities, competitions, demonstrations, and interactive sessions related to traditional day.

Event Date: November 61, 2026
Venue: Main Auditorium
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Students can register individually or in teams and participate in the activities planned by the organizing committee.
The organizing committee will manage registrations, schedules, participant coordination, and venue arrangements.
The event also includes opportunities for students to interact, demonstrate their skills, and learn from other participants.

Traditional Day provides students with an opportunity to develop confidence, teamwork, creativity, and practical skills.
