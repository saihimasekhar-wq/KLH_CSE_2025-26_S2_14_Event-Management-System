WEB DEVELOPMENT WORKSHOP 2026

The Web Development Workshop is a practical learning event for students interested in web technologies.
The workshop introduces participants to modern frontend and backend development concepts.

Event Date: October 20, 2026
Venue: Web Technology Lab
Time: 11:00 AM onwards

Students can practice HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and basic server development.
The workshop team will manage registrations, lab systems, and instructor sessions.
Participants will build a small web application during the workshop.

The workshop provides students with an opportunity to strengthen practical development and teamwork skills.
